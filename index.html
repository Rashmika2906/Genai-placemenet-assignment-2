What I'm doing right now, and why.

I'm a third-year Computer Science Engineering student, and most of my learning happens outside the syllabus — picking an idea, reading until I actually understand why each piece of the stack exists, then building it from scratch instead of copying a reference implementation.

That's meant everything from a resume analyser powered by Gemini, to a meeting summarizer that transcribes and reasons about audio locally. I'm currently deep in data structures & algorithms prep while continuing to ship independent AI projects, with the goal of landing an internship at a top-tier tech company.
